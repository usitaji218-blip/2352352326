    Wallet screening rules

    To keep the distribution fair, wallet activity is reviewed before final allocations are approved.

    • 🐾Snapshot taken at a random block inside the window
    • 🐾Minimum hold of $20 for at least 14 days
    • 🐾One allocation per wallet cluster, not per wallet
    • 🐾Exchange / custodial / bridge addresses excluded
    • 🐾Coordinated clusters are filtered out before distribution

    Official claim safety

    The Season 1 claim is for eligible $ZCAT holders only. It never asks for your seed phrase, private key, or any kind of wallet validation.
